About

I'm a Nursing student at Ateneo de Davao University, and outside of clinical coursework I put together visual and communication projects — infographics, event materials, and this small personal brand.

My tagline, Nursing – Care – Passion – Precise, is the short version of how I try to work: attentive to people, genuinely invested, and exact about the details — a habit that carries over from the ward to the page.

The monogram on this site is my personal logo: an M and A built from two intersecting strokes, meant to read as steady and a little hand-drawn rather than corporate.

Selected work

Class projects & design work

Activity 1 · Event Poster, GE 4120

Free Coffee — Arrupe Hall, Booth 7

A poster for a free-coffee giveaway booth, designed to be readable from a distance across a hallway and to feel warm rather than corporate.

Why I made this This was my first activity for GE 4120, so the brief was simple: get people to stop at the booth. I leaned into the coffee-splash imagery and a friendly mascot because a giveaway table should feel inviting, not like an ad.

Activity 2 · Brand Guide, GE 4120

Personal Brand: Color, Type & Mood

The palette, fonts, and mood board behind my personal brand — the same system used across this site.

Why I made this Before I could design anything else, I needed a consistent visual language. I chose warm browns and a single orange accent because they read as grounded and human rather than clinical-white, which felt more honest to what nursing actually looks like day to day.

Activity 3 · Infographic, GE 4120

Effects of Procrastination on Academic Performance

An infographic breaking down why students procrastinate, the short- and long-term effects, and six concrete steps to work against it — from starting small to setting realistic deadlines.

Why I made this Procrastination is something I see constantly in myself and my classmates, especially with clinical requirements stacked on top of lecture work. I wanted a reference that was honest about the short-term relief it gives, not just the long-term cost, since that's the actual reason it's hard to stop.
