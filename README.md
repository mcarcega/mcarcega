<b>Personal Brand: Color, Type & Mood</b>
<br><br>
The palette, fonts, and mood board behind my personal brand — the same system used across this site.
<br><br>
<sub><b>Why I made this</b> — Before I could design anything else, I needed a consistent visual language. I chose warm browns and a single orange accent because they read as grounded and human rather than clinical-white, which felt more honest to what nursing actually looks like day to day.</sub>

<b>Effects of Procrastination on Academic Performance</b>

An infographic breaking down why students procrastinate, the short- and long-term effects, and six concrete steps to work against it.
<br><br>
<sub><b>Why I made this</b> — Procrastination is something I see constantly in myself and my classmates, especially with clinical requirements stacked on top of lecture work. I wanted a reference that was honest about the short-term relief it gives, not just the long-term cost.</sub>
