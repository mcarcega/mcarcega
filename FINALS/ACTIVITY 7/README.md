📄 ACTIVITY 7 · RESUME COMPARISON

GE 4120 — AI-Assisted Resume Design: ChatGPT vs. Gemini

A comparison of AI-generated resume designs using the same personal information, formatting instructions, color palette, and typography preferences. The activity explores how ChatGPT and Gemini interpret design prompts, organize professional information, and create resumes tailored to a nursing student.

---

📋 Project Overview

- Project: Resume Generation & AI Comparison
- Course: GE 4120
- Tools: ChatGPT & Gemini
- Focus: Resume formatting, AI-assisted design, and output comparison
- Design Palette: Night ink, marigold, blush, teal, and paper
- Typography: Bricolage Grotesque & Figtree

💡 Why I Made This

I wanted to explore how different AI tools could transform the same personal information into a professional resume while following a specific visual identity. By comparing ChatGPT and Gemini, I was able to observe their differences in design, organization, accuracy, and ability to follow instructions.

This activity helped me understand that AI can make resume creation faster and more accessible, but its output still requires human review to ensure that the information is accurate, meaningful, and genuinely reflects the person behind it.

🖌️ Design Approach

- 🎨 Consistent color palette — used night ink, marigold, blush, teal, and paper to create a distinct and approachable visual identity.
- 🔤 Typography pairing — combined Bricolage Grotesque for expressive headlines and Figtree for readable body text.
- 📑 Structured layout — organized personal information, educational background, and professional details for easier scanning.
- 🩺 Health communication principles — emphasized plain language, clear hierarchy, and accessible presentation.
- 🤖 AI comparison — evaluated the strengths and limitations of ChatGPT and Gemini in resume creation.

🎯 Key Takeaway

AI is most useful when treated as a collaborative tool rather than a replacement for personal judgment. It can help structure, refine, and present information efficiently, but authenticity, accuracy, critical thinking, and ownership must remain with the user.
