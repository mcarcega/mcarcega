💻 ACTIVITY 4 · IT ELECTIVE PRESENTATION

GE 4120 — Google Docs: Creating, Collaborating & Managing Documents

An educational presentation about Google Docs, exploring its essential features, functions, and role in modern digital productivity. It covers document creation, formatting, organization, collaboration, sharing, and responsible use of digital tools.

---

📋 Project Overview

- Project: IT Elective Presentation
- Course: GE 4120
- Topic: Google Docs
- Focus: Digital literacy, document management, and collaboration
- Format: Discussion, live demonstration, and interactive quiz

💡 Why I Made This

I wanted to present Google Docs beyond just being an online word processor, but as a tool that helps students work smarter, stay organized, and collaborate more efficiently. Through this activity, I aimed to demonstrate how its features can be applied to academic tasks, group projects, and everyday document management.

🖥️ Presentation Approach

- 📄 Practical discussion — explained the purpose, features, and functions of Google Docs.
- 🧑‍💻 Live demonstration — showed how to use its tools in actual document workflows.
- 🤝 Collaboration — highlighted sharing, comments, suggesting mode, and real-time teamwork.
- 📝 Interactive quiz — reinforced learning through a short knowledge-check activity.
- 🔐 Digital responsibility — emphasized proper sharing permissions, privacy, and responsible AI use.

---

Technology is more than knowing the tools—it's knowing how to use them effectively, responsibly, and collaboratively. 💻
