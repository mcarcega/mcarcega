
🎨 ACTIVITY 2 · BRAND GUIDE

GE 4120 — Personal Brand: Color, Type & Mood

A personal brand guide exploring the colors, typography, and overall mood behind my visual identity. This guide establishes the design system used across my website, keeping every element consistent and recognizable.

---

🎨 Project Overview

- Project: Personal Brand Guide
- Course: GE 4120
- Focus: Color palette, typography, and mood board
- Purpose: Establish a consistent visual identity across personal projects and website design.

💡 Why I Made This

Before I could design anything else, I needed a consistent visual language that would reflect my personality and the way I wanted my work to feel.

I chose warm browns with a single orange accent because they feel grounded, approachable, and human rather than clinical-white. I wanted my visual identity to reflect what nursing actually looks like day to day—not just a profession associated with clean hospital spaces, but one built around empathy, warmth, patience, and genuine connection.

🖌️ Design Approach

- 🤎 Warm brown palette — communicates groundedness, comfort, and warmth.
- 🟠 Orange accent — adds energy and personality without overwhelming the design.
- 🔤 Typography — chosen to support readability and a consistent visual identity.
- 🌤️ Mood and atmosphere — human, warm, honest, and approachable.
- 🧩 Consistency — the same design system is carried across my website and creative work.

🎯 Design Philosophy

I wanted my personal brand to feel less clinical and more human. The colors and typography are not just aesthetic choices; they help express the kind of person and future healthcare professional I want to be—someone who values warmth, authenticity, and connection.

---

Designing a visual identity that feels like me: grounded, warm, and human. 🤎
