
🌐 ACTIVITY 6 · PERSONAL WEBSITE

GE 4120 — Personal Portfolio: A Practice of Care

A personal website designed to reflect my identity, values, and growth as a nursing student. Built around warm neutral tones, deep burgundy, and muted orange accents, it presents a professional yet human-centered approach to nursing, learning, and personal development.

---

🎨 Project Overview

- Project: Personal Website / Digital Portfolio
- Course: GE 4120
- Focus: Personal identity, nursing philosophy, learning, and professional growth
- Theme: Care, passion, precision, and room to grow

💡 Why I Made This

I created this website to have a space that represents who I am beyond academics—a place to showcase my values, growth, and the kind of healthcare professional I hope to become. A personal website serves as a digital portfolio that makes my experiences, projects, and skills accessible while helping me prepare for the transition from student life into the professional world.

I wanted the design to express a quiet commitment to learning with humility, approaching care with attention, and becoming more precise without losing sight of the person.

🖌️ Design Approach

- 🤎 Warm neutral palette — creates a calm, grounded, and thoughtful atmosphere.
- 🍷 Deep burgundy & orange accents — add character, contrast, and subtle emphasis.
- 🔤 Editorial typography — gives the website a refined, clean, and professional feel.
- 🩺 Whole-person care — reflects the importance of noticing, understanding, and supporting others.
- 🌱 Personal growth — highlights learning as an ongoing process of reflection and development.

🎯 Design Philosophy

A practice of care begins with how we see.

This website reflects my belief that nursing is a discipline of both knowledge and humanity. It is a reminder to keep learning with humility, meet care with attention, and continue growing without losing sight of the person in front of me.

---

Care, passion, precision—and room to grow. 🤎
