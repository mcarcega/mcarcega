☕ ACTIVITY 1 · EVENT POSTER

GE 4120 — Free Coffee | Arrupe Hall, Booth 7

A poster design for a free coffee giveaway booth, created to catch attention from across a hallway while keeping the overall look warm, inviting, and approachable rather than corporate.

---

🎨 Project Overview

- Project: Event Poster
- Course: GE 4120
- Event: Free Coffee Giveaway
- Location: Arrupe Hall, Booth 7
- Design Goal: Grab attention from a distance and encourage people to stop by.

💡 Why I Made This

This was my first activity for GE 4120, and the brief was simple: get people to stop at the booth.

I leaned into coffee-splash imagery and a friendly mascot to create a design that feels fun, welcoming, and approachable. Instead of making the poster feel like a corporate advertisement, I wanted it to communicate the excitement of a simple giveaway and make people feel invited to drop by for a free cup of coffee.

🖌️ Design Approach

- ☕ Coffee-splash imagery — adds energy and makes the poster visually engaging.
- 🐻 Friendly mascot — gives the design personality and makes it feel welcoming.
- 👀 Distance readability — keeps the main message easy to notice in a busy hallway.
- 🤎 Warm visual tone — creates a cozy, casual, and inviting atmosphere.

---

Sometimes, a simple cup of coffee is enough to bring people together. ☕
